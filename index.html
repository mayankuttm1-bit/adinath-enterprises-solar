Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum subsidy available under PM Surya Ghar Yojana? expand_more

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential households get ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. In Uttar Pradesh, an additional state subsidy of up to ₹30,000 is also applicable for eligible consumers.

Do I need to visit KESCo office for net-metering approvals? expand_more

No! Adinath Enterprises handles 100% of the online registration, document verification, site inspection scheduling, and bi-directional net-meter installation with KESCo. You do not need to make any office runs.

How much rooftop space is required for a 3 kW solar system? expand_more

A typical 3 kW solar plant requires approximately 250 to 300 square feet of shadow-free rooftop area. We use elevated hot-dip galvanized mounting structures so your rooftop space remains usable underneath.

What happens during rainy days or Kanpur winter fog? expand_more

Modern Monocrystalline TopCon panels continue generating power even in diffused light conditions. Since the system is connected via KESCo net-metering, any additional units needed are seamlessly drawn from the grid without interruption.
