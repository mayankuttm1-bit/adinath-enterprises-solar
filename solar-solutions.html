Got Questions?

Solar Solutions FAQ

How is the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy received? expand_more

The subsidy is not a paper discount—it is credited directly to the beneficiary bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) within 30 days of bidirectional net-meter installation and commissioning by KESCo/UPNEDA.

Can solar run during a power grid outage? expand_more

Standard on-grid net-metered solar systems automatically shut down during grid outages for lineman anti-islanding safety. However, Adinath Solar provides hybrid inverter setups with lithium battery storage or DG synchronization controllers that keep your essential loads powered 24/7.

How does the 40% accelerated depreciation benefit commercial businesses? expand_more

Under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, solar equipment is eligible for 40% depreciation in year one. If your business pays 25% or 30% corporate income tax, this effectively reduces your initial capital outlay by 10%–12% in immediate tax savings, accelerating your payback period to under 3 years.

What documents are required to initiate net-metering in Kanpur? expand_more

You only need your recent KESCo / DVVNL electricity bill (with matching sanctioned load), property ownership document / Registry or House Tax receipt, Aadhaar card, and a cancelled bank cheque for DBT subsidy. Our team prepares and files all portal documentation on your behalf.
